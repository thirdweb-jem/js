# What is Connect?

thirdweb Connect offers an efficient and flexible solution for connecting your app or game to web3, offering:

- [External wallet connections](https://portal.thirdweb.com/connect/wallet/sign-in-methods/external-wallets) to over 500+ EVM compatible wallets
- Customizable In-app wallets & ecosystem wallets to help you build unique branded experiences.
- [Smart Wallets](https://portal.thirdweb.com/connect/account-abstraction/overview), for sponsoring user transactions for users to make your app accessible.
- A [reliable web3 payment solution](https://portal.thirdweb.com/connect/pay/overview) for monetizing your app, including onramping, bridging + swapping—using crypto or credit cards.
- Flexible web2 and [web3 authentication](https://portal.thirdweb.com/connect/auth) flows, to let users sign in with the email, phone, passkeys or social accounts.
- A [performant blockchain API](https://portal.thirdweb.com/connect/blockchain-api) for performing actions such as connecting and disconnecting wallets, viewing balance, displaying ENS names, and executing transactions.

## Supported Chains
